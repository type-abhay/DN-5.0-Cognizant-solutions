Parameters:
 - Initial Investment (PV): $1000.0
 - Annual Growth Rate (r): 7.000000000000001%
 - Forecast Periods (n): 10 years

Predicted Future Value (Iterative): $1967.15

--- Recursive Algorithm Analysis ---
1. Concept of Recursion:
   Recursion simplifies complex problems by breaking them down into smaller self-similar sub-problems until reaching a base condition.

2. Time & Space Complexity:
   - Simple Linear Recursion: O(n) time complexity and O(n) call stack space memory.
   - Tree Recursion (if branch factors increase): can jump up to O(2^n) exponential complexity if computing subproblems repeatedly.

3. Optimization Techniques:
   - Memoization: Caching previously calculated recursive results to avoid redundant computations.
   - Tail-Call Optimization / Iteration: Converting recursion to an iterative loop reduces memory overhead down to O(1) space.
